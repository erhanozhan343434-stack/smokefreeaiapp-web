How to Prepare Your Home Before You Quit Smoking

Before your quit day, take one pass through your home to clear out smoking supplies. Start with the places where you used to reach for a cigarette, then check the spots where a spare pack or lighter might have ended up. A short checklist can make the job easier to finish in one evening.

Start at your usual smoking spot

If you smoked on the porch, check the table, the shelf by the door, and the jacket you wore outside. If you usually smoked by the kitchen window, check the sill and nearby drawers. The U.S. National Cancer Institute recommends getting rid of cigarettes, lighters, and ashtrays when you quit. Use that list as you move from room to room.

Check the places you used to keep a spare

  • Entryway: Look in coat pockets, bags, and the bowl where you drop your keys.
  • Living room: Check the side table and any drawer where you kept an extra lighter.
  • Porch or balcony: Clear the table and check the cubby or shelf by the door.

Give one spot a new purpose

Pick the spot you’re most likely to revisit tomorrow. Put something there that fits what you’d like to do instead. For example, if the porch table used to hold an ashtray, clear the table and set your book by the door. When you step outside after dinner, you’ll have something ready to do.

If you share the space with someone who smokes, focus on your own supplies and agree on what to do with shared areas. You could ask, “Can we keep the porch table clear of cigarettes and lighters this week?”

For more ideas on preparing for the first day, see the National Cancer Institute’s quit-day guide for U.S. readers.
