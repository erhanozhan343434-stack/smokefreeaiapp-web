How to Track Your Cigarette Savings One Week at a Time

To track cigarette spending you’ve avoided, start with your own recent spending. Pick a typical week before you quit, write down what you usually bought, and compare that amount with what you actually spent on cigarettes this week. Keep the record simple enough to update once a week.

Choose a useful starting amount

Use a recent receipt or the price you actually paid. If you usually bought three packs each week, record that quantity and the price per pack. If your spending varied, look at several typical weeks and note that your starting amount is approximate. A national average may differ from the price at your usual store.

Try a clearly labeled example

Suppose someone previously bought three packs a week at $10 per pack. Their usual weekly cigarette spending was $30. If they bought no cigarettes this week, their estimated avoided spending for that week is $30. These are made-up numbers to demonstrate the calculation. Replace them with your own purchases and prices.

Record what happened that week

If you bought cigarettes, include that purchase in the actual-spending line. For instance, a $10 purchase against the same $30 starting amount leaves $20 in estimated avoided spending. Keep earlier entries so you can review the record over time.

Track any money you deliberately set aside in another column. Avoided cigarette spending and the amount you transfer to savings can differ, especially if you use some of the money for other expenses. Recording both lets you see where the money went.

Give the next weekly check a place in your routine

Choose a recurring moment, such as Sunday evening when you review receipts. Add the week’s figures and one note about what you’d like to use the money for. You might choose a book, a day out, or simply more room in your weekly budget.

The National Cancer Institute’s Smokefree.gov offers a quit-smoking savings calculator for U.S. readers. Its craving-management guide also suggests adding up cigarette savings and deciding how to use them. These government resources are separate from the SmokeFree AI app.
